A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

Can I make a booking on this website?

You can submit a booking request in this demo. The request is saved to a local SQLite database; it does not contact a real property.

Are the prices real-time?

No. Listings and prices are sample portfolio data and should be replaced with verified provider information.

Can I save favourite stays?

Yes. Tap the heart icon on a stay card. Favourites are stored in this browser using localStorage.
